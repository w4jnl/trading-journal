Copy-trade reconciliation

Check whether a follower account mirrored a leader at a multiple over a date range. It compares gross buy and sell volume per symbol (not net position, which reads 0 whenever both accounts are flat), so a missing, extra, or partial copied fill shows up even after everything has closed. Keep the range to a single day - the copy factor is only stable within a session.
