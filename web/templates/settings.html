Hidden accounts stay fully visible in Accounts and still import; picking one explicitly in the Trades / Calendar / Analytics account filter overrides this.

IBKR live sync

Trades sync from your self-hosted Client Portal Gateway(s) — one per IB login. The gateway must be logged in via its own browser page (2FA); the app keeps the session alive and pulls the rolling 7-day trade window. History older than the cutover stays with MotiveWave CSV imports; newer MotiveWave rows for live-synced accounts are skipped automatically to prevent double-counting.
