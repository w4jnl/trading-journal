[trading-journal] how the parts connect
one Go binary and one SQLite file; everything else is a client of it or a feed into it
clients

browser

server-rendered pages, html/template + HTMX partials; polls /data/version every 10 s and swaps the live regions in place

iPhone and iPad app

SwiftUI, in app/ios: /api/v1 with a device key and the SSE stream; nudges and the Session Live Activity arrive by push; the last answers are kept for offline

AI assistants

Claude Code, claude.ai, ChatGPT over MCP at /mcp: 21 tools to read trades and stats and to write notes, tags and plans; an API key or OAuth 2.1

scripts and other tools

REST /api/v1 with a tj_ key; OpenAPI at /api/v1/openapi.json

who is who

local sign-in with sessions, or a reverse proxy's headers; keys and devices per user; several users, one instance

one Go binary · cmd/server

internal/handlers

pages and HTMX partials · /api/v1 JSON with the SSE /events · /mcp and the OAuth 2.1 server · settings, admin, pairing

journal

one write path for notes: pages, API, MCP and the AI reviewer

positions

FIFO builder: fills become trades; flips, partial closes, commissions

importer

CSV watch folder and upload, MW e-mail; topstepx and ibkr sync

copyset, dayplan

one decision across accounts; the plan before the open

guardrail · push

coach rules and nudges as fills arrive, pushed to the phone

ai

note reviews, day runs, plan and level drafts

marketdata

bars, chart PNGs, MFE/MAE, the stop & target lab

gex · gexview

gamma regime, flip and walls on trades and the GEX pages

livedata, backup

a change version per user for poll and SSE; nightly tar.gz

SQLite · data/trading.db

modernc.org/sqlite, pure Go, no CGO; migrations embedded and applied at boot; sqlc-typed queries. Executions are the record, trades and daily stats derive.

files

data/attachments, imports/processed, backups/*.tar.gz

pages

dashboard · trades and copy sets · open positions with their plans · journal, day plan, lessons, digest · calendar · analytics and the stop & target lab · accounts and equity · GEX · OI · import · settings
