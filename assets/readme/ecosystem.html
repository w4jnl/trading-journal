w4j-mw-extensions

Java studies and strategies for the MotiveWave SDK: DailyAtrLevels, VwapBands, GexLevels, which draws gex-engine's levels and reports the futures price back

charts and orders

Rithmic and CQG accounts (Apex, Lucid, AMP), IBKR; exports fills as CSV, mails each fill

soon

mwrelay

store-and-forward SMTP relay on the trading Mac: takes MotiveWave's fill e-mails, keeps them in SQLite, delivers each to every journal at least once, over SMTP or an SSH tunnel. mwrelay-bar shows the queue and the day's P&L in the menu bar.

brokers with an API

TopstepX / ProjectX: trades, fees and exact-contract bars. IBKR Client Portal Gateway: executions. Both synced on a schedule by the journal itself.
